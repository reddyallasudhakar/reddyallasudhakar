### 🛡️ What I'm Building

**[fairClaims](https://fairclaims.policygaido.com)**: an AI-driven platform that helps policyholders recover rejected or short-settled health insurance claims.

- 🧠 **GaidoAI orchestration:** Specialised agents (intake, onboarding, document, rebuttal, action center, closure) run a case end to end
- 📑 **Claims analysis pipeline:** policy parsing, hospital network classification, and a targeted rebuttal builder grounded in policy wording and regulations
- 🔁 **claims state machine:** a single source of truth across 8 stages, from intake to the Ombudsman escalation ladder
- 💬 **WhatsApp AI agent:** conversational case intake and updates, wired into CRM
- ⏱️ **Action Center:** submission tracking against IRDAI regulatory timelines

**[Policygaido Advisory](https://policygaido.com)**: commission-neutral insurance guidance, with an AI content pipeline that decodes the fine print.
