# Hi, I'm Sudhakar 👋

I build AI agents that fight for policyholders: recovering rejected health insurance claims and giving honest, commission-neutral insurance advice in India.

Most of my work is hands-on: multi-agent orchestration, WhatsApp and voice agents, state machines for long-running workflows, and evals for high-stakes LLM outputs where a weak argument costs someone real money.

I'm building [fairClaims](https://fairclaims.policygaido.com), where a team of AI agents runs a claim dispute end to end, from reading the rejection letter to escalating with the insurer, the grievance officer, IRDAI and the Ombudsman. And whoever who get frustrated with their current policy can also get a new one at [Advisory](https://policygaido.com)

**What I work on**

- 🤖 AI Agents: multi-agent orchestration with LangGraph, WhatsApp agents, voice agents, internal ops bots
- 📑 Claims intelligence: policy parsing → rejection analysis → counter adguments -> targeted rebuttals → escalation -> CRM automation
- 🔁 Agentic workflows: claims state machine across 8 stages as the single source of truth
- 📚 RAG setups: Over policy wordings, regulations and case references
- 💬 Omnichannel agents: WhatsApp (WATI), voice (Exotel) and email, all feeding one case state
- 🧪 Evals: turning real case reviews into eval specs for LLM-generated arguments
- ☁️ Stack: Typescript, Node, React, LangGraph, LangSmith, Supabase
